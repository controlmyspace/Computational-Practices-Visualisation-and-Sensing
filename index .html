Part 3 — The sweet spot

Not all screen time is equal.

Scroll through the story below — each step reveals a new line on the chart, built with D3.js.

01

Start with learning 📚

When students use devices to study, scores actually rise — peaking around 1–2 hours a day, then plateauing. A little structured screen time helps.

Peak score: ~481 at ≤1hr vs 456 with no device use at all.

02

Add gaming 🎮

Gaming follows a similar early rise — up to 1–3hrs is actually linked to higher scores than no gaming at all. But the drop after 3hrs is sharp.

Best score: ~496 at ≤1hr. Drops to 441 at 7+hrs.

03

Now social media 📲

Social media follows a similar arc early on — but the decline is the steepest of all activities. The heaviest users score dramatically lower than moderate users.

Drops ~72 points from the ≤1hr peak (501) to 7+hrs (429).

04

The full picture 🌐

All four lines together reveal the pattern: there's a sweet spot around 1 hour, after which more time consistently means lower scores — for every activity type.

The green zone marks the sweet spot. Hover any dot for exact scores.
